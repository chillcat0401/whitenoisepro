## Requirements
### Requirement: Memorable brand promise
WhiteNoisePro SHALL use a concise brand promise that is memorable, low-distraction, and suitable for sleep and focus contexts.

#### Scenario: User sees primary brand copy
- **WHEN** the user sees the app's primary brand copy in app or store materials
- **THEN** the copy MUST include or align with the promise “把世界调低一点。”
- **AND** it MUST communicate calm, local, low-distraction sound without claiming medical or guaranteed sleep outcomes

### Requirement: App copy follows quiet brand voice
WhiteNoisePro SHALL use a quiet, concrete, policy-safe Chinese brand voice for key in-app copy.

#### Scenario: Home copy is rendered
- **WHEN** the Home screen renders
- **THEN** it MUST include a short supporting line that reinforces the brand promise
- **AND** the line MUST NOT crowd the top bar or require multi-step onboarding

#### Scenario: Recommendation copy is rendered
- **WHEN** the app presents timer or sound recommendations
- **THEN** the copy MUST describe the recommendation gently and concretely
- **AND** it MUST avoid implying that the app guarantees sleep or treats sleep conditions

### Requirement: Store copy uses brand before features
Google Play listing copy SHALL lead with the brand promise before listing app features.

#### Scenario: Store listing draft is prepared
- **WHEN** release-readiness store copy is updated
- **THEN** the short description and opening paragraph MUST include a distinctive brand-led message
- **AND** feature lists MUST remain accurate to the current release
- **AND** policy-sensitive disclaimers MUST remain present
